# AUTHORING STYLE GUIDE — "Digital Banking and Financial Technology: The Malaysian Perspective"

**Book title:** Digital Banking and Financial Technology: The Malaysian Perspective
**Audience:** Diploma and undergraduate students (Malaysian polytechnics, community colleges, university foundation/first-year). Assume no prior finance or IT background. Define every technical term at first use.
**Language:** Clear, plain academic English (CEFR B2 readable). Short paragraphs (3–6 sentences). Use tables and bullet lists to aid scanning.
**Voice:** Formal-textbook but friendly. Address students as "you" sparingly. Avoid hype; stay balanced (benefits AND risks).
**Format:** Markdown. Chapter starts at `# Chapter N: Title`. Sections `## N.1 ...`, subsections `### N.1.1 ...`. Numbering MUST match the assigned outline exactly.

## MANDATORY CHAPTER STRUCTURE (in this order)

1. `# Chapter N: Title`
2. **Chapter Outline** — bulleted list of the N.x sections.
3. **Learning Outcomes** — 6 outcomes, each tagged with its Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive level, using action verbs. Cover at least four different levels across Remember / Understand / Apply / Analyse / Evaluate / Create. Format:
   `1. **Define** ... *(Bloom's level: Remember)*`
4. **Opening Vignette** — a short (120–180 words) realistic Malaysian scenario featuring a named fictional student/shopkeeper/SME owner that motivates the chapter.
5. **Sections N.1 … N.x** — exactly the sections in the assigned outline, each expanded with 2–4 `###` subsections. Every section must:
   - include at least one in-text APA 7 citation; **all formal definitions MUST be cited** (e.g., "Financial technology (FinTech) is defined as ... (Puschmann, 2017)");
   - interleave pedagogical boxes (see below);
   - connect global theory to Malaysian practice.
6. **Pedagogical boxes** (insert throughout the chapter, minimums): 3× Quick Note, 2× Did You Know?, 2× Brainstormer, 1× Malaysian Spotlight. Format as blockquotes:
   `> **📌 QUICK NOTE:** one-to-three sentence clarification of a tricky point.`
   `> **💡 DID YOU KNOW? (TRIVIA):** a surprising, factual titbit.`
   `> **🧠 BRAINSTORMER:** 1–2 open-ended critical-thinking questions (no answers given).`
   `> **🇲🇾 MALAYSIAN SPOTLIGHT:** short paragraph on a local institution, initiative or statistic.`
7. **Case Study** — `## Case Study N.1: Title` (may add N.2). A 250–450 word factual Malaysian case (use only facts from the fact bank or well-known public facts), followed by 3–4 **Discussion Questions**.
8. `## Chapter Summary` — 8–12 bullet points keyed to sections.
9. `## Key Terms` — glossary entries, exactly in this format (one per line):
   `- **Term**: concise definition (15–40 words).`
   Provide 12–18 terms that appear in bold in the chapter.
10. `## Test Your Knowledge`
    - **Part A: Multiple Choice** — 10 MCQs, options A–D, testing several Bloom's levels.
    - **Part B: Short Answer** — 5 structured questions.
    - `### Answer Guide` — MCQ key with one-line rationale each; 2–4 sentence model answers for Part B.
11. `## Problem-Solving Corner` — 2–3 applied problems (calculation or scenario analysis) with fully worked **Solutions**. Numbers must be internally consistent and realistic (use RM).
12. `## References` — APA 7th edition, alphabetical. Only real sources; prefer the master reference list (references-master.md).

## CITATION RULES (APA 7)
- Parenthetical: (Arner et al., 2016) / (Bank Negara Malaysia [BNM], 2020) — first BNM citation spelt out, then (BNM, 2020).
- Narrative: Puschmann (2017) argues that ...
- Two authors always: (Lee & Shin, 2018). Three or more: et al. from first citation.
- Government/regulator documents: (Securities Commission Malaysia [SC], 2024) then (SC, 2024).
- Every statistic must have a citation from the fact bank.

## FACTUAL DISCIPLINE
- Use ONLY statistics found in `malaysia-facts.md` (verified) or widely known textbook facts (e.g., Bitcoin's 21 million cap).
- Do NOT invent market sizes, launch dates, fee percentages of real firms, or project names. Where uncertain, write qualitatively ("industry fees typically range around ..." only if in fact bank).
- First use of an abbreviation: spell it out, e.g., "electronic know-your-customer (e-KYC)".

## STYLE NOTES
- Currency: RM (Malaysian ringgit). Percentages with % sign.
- Bold key terms at first definition; italics for emphasis only.
- Tables: use Markdown tables with headers.
- Target length: 7,000–9,000 words per chapter. Do not pad; depth over repetition.
